# Staff guide

## First time: set up your PIN
1. Open the link your manager texted or emailed you. Tip: add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
2. Choose **Phone + PIN**, then **Set up my PIN**.
3. Enter the mobile number your manager has on file and pick a 4-digit PIN. That is your login from now on.

Some teams sign in with a work email instead. Use whichever your manager set up.

## Forgot your PIN
Tap **Forgot PIN**, enter your mobile number, and you will get a text with a code. Enter the code and choose a new PIN.

## Clock in
1. Sign in.
2. Pick your **Location** and your **Role** (for example Stylist or Front Desk).
3. Tap **Clock In**. You will see a clock running and your recent punches.

If you work two roles in one day, clock out of the first and clock in again with the other role. Pay can differ by role, so this matters.

The app may ask to use your location when you punch. It only saves where the punch happened.

## Clock out
Tap **Clock Out** when you finish, including before a long break if your team asks you to.

No signal? Tap anyway. The punch is saved on your phone and sent as soon as you are back online.

## My Hours
Shows **This week** and **Last week**, with a day-by-day list of hours.

## My Time Card
Shows **This period** and **Last period** with each day, location, role and total.

## Ask for a fix (correction)
Forgot to clock in or out, or picked the wrong location or role?
1. Tap **Request a correction** (or use My Time Card).
2. Enter the right time or details and a short reason.
3. Send. A manager approves or turns it down. Approved fixes show up on your time card.

## Reminders
If you are still clocked in many hours later (12 hours unless your manager changes it), you may get a text reminding you to clock out.
If a shift runs past midnight, you will get a text the next morning asking for your real clock-out time. Those hours do not count until fixed.
